# MultiVision Plan5: coordinate-aware physical geometry overlays

Source: [ADR-0003: Physical Geometry Overlays](../docs/ADR-0003_%20Physical%20Geometry%20Overlays.md), constrained by the implemented shared metric calibration in Plan4 and the existing camera/projector geometry, session and display contracts. Plan5 adds projector-native, camera-pixel and calibrated physical geometry requests for grids, circles, rects, lines and rulers; it does not add game rules, piece tracking or AI logic.

The Driver-approved mixed-coordinate decisions are recorded in the [shared validation contract](plan5-validation-contract.md). Do not modify ADR-0001, ADR-0002, ADR-0003 or `harness.toml`.

1. Record the Plan5 implementation boundary in `docs/physical-geometry-overlays-implementation.md`: projector-native geometry is the final rendering authority; requests may use `camera_px`, `projector_px`, `surface_mm` or derived `surface_edge_mm`; anchor and shape/measurement spaces may differ; physical quantities require usable metric calibration; camera-native quantities require the selected camera's usable calibration; overlay state is session-local, named optionally, independently visible and coexistence-safe; preserve the existing point and metric-ruler compatibility paths; verify the document links the [shared validation contract](plan5-validation-contract.md) and contains no unapproved game semantics.
